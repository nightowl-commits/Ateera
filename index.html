Who we are

Enactus is a global community of university students who build real ventures to solve real problems — using entrepreneurship rather than charity as the tool. Enactus ZHDC is the chapter at Zakir Husain Delhi College, University of Delhi, where a student team designs, launches and runs social enterprises that create income for the people they work with.

Our flagship venture right now is Project Ateera, but the team behind it also mentors new project ideas, competes at Enactus India events, and trains members in the practical side of running a small business: sourcing, pricing, operations, and telling a venture's story.

Project Ateera

A candle business built around a person, not just a product.

The mission

Project Ateera partners with local community artisans — many of whom had no prior access to a steady income — and builds a proper small business around a craft they're taught to master: hand-pouring scented candles. Every candle sold puts money directly back into that partnership.

Why candles

Candle-making is low-cost to start, teachable in a short time, and sells year-round — which makes it a realistic first step into entrepreneurship rather than a one-off craft project. It also gives us room to experiment with scents and packaging rooted in local, familiar ingredients.

What we handle

The Enactus ZHDC team supports the parts a new small business usually struggles with: sourcing raw material, product design, pricing, packaging, and selling — on campus, at pop-ups, and online — so our partners can focus on the craft.

How a candle gets made

  1. 1
    Source

    Wax, wicks and fragrance oils are sourced with cost and quality both in mind, keeping the business sustainable long-term.

  2. 2
    Pour

    Our community partners hand-pour and finish every candle, a skill built up through hands-on training with the team.

  3. 3
    Sell

    Enactus ZHDC handles pricing, packaging and sales, and routes the proceeds back to the people who made them.
